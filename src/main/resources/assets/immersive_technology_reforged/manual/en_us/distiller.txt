Distiller
Clean water and salt
<&multiblock>The Distiller processes water into Distilled Water, with salt available as a byproduct.

Use it when a recipe calls for a cleaner water input, or when you want salt without relying on other resource paths.<np>

    - Extract Distilled Water from the fluid output.
    - Pull salt from the item output.
