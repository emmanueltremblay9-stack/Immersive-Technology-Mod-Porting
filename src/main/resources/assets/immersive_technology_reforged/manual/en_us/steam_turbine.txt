Steam Turbine
Pressure into rotation
<&multiblock>The Steam Turbine consumes steam and produces rotational power for machines such as the Alternator.

Exhaust steam exits through the fluid output and can be cooled back into water in a Cooling Tower.<np>

§lOperation§r
    - Pipe steam into the fluid input.
    - Connect a mechanical consumer to the rotor output.
    - JEI lists valid steam recipes, including this mod's steam and tagged steam from other mods.
