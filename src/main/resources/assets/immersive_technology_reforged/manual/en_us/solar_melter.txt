Solar Melting Crucible
Solar heat to molten fluids
<&multiblock>The Solar Melting Crucible uses reflected sunlight to process already-heated slurry fluids into molten outputs.

It is the high-temperature end of the solar processing chain, not a general solid-item melter.<np>

§lOperation§r
    - Pipe heated slurry fluids into the front input.
    - Pull molten fluids from the back output.
    - JEI lists the required temperature and valid Solar Melting Crucible recipes.
    - Keep Solar Tower and Solar Melting Crucible bases about 44 blocks apart; the in-game warning gives the exact move distance if one is too close.
