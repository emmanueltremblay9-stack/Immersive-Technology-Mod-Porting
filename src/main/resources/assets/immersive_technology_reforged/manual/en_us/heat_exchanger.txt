Heat Exchanger
Move heat between fluids
<&multiblock>The Heat Exchanger transfers heat from a hot fluid into another fluid. In a steam setup, it can use hot fluids such as flue gas or molten salt to turn water or distilled water into steam.

This is useful when you want to recover heat from a Gas Turbine loop or move solar heat into steam production.<np>

§lOperation§r
    - Pipe the hot fluid into the hot-side input.
    - Pipe the fluid to heat into the cold-side input.
    - Extract both outputs from their matching sides.
    - JEI lists which hot fluids, cold fluids, and outputs are valid.
