Gas Turbine
Liquid fuel into rotation
<&multiblock>The Gas Turbine burns liquid fuel to create hot, high-velocity gas. That gas turns the turbine blades and provides rotational power for machines such as the Alternator.

Flue gas exits below the rotor and can be used as a hot fluid in the Heat Exchanger.<np>

§lOperation§r
    - Pipe valid liquid fuel into the fuel input; JEI lists supported fuels.
    - The MV energy input is the spark plug and may stay powered.
    - The HV energy input is the starter and only needs power until ignition.
    - Attach a mechanical consumer to the rotor output for the turbine to run usefully.
