Steel Fluid Tank
Bulk fluid storage
<&multiblock>The Steel Fluid Tank is a large multiblock fluid store for Immersive Technology production lines.

Use it as a buffer for steam, water, fuels, slurry fluids, molten salt, or other compatible fluids. It is storage and IO, not a machine that converts steam into exhaust steam.<np>

§lOperation§r
    - Pipe fluids into the tank through valid input ports.
    - Extract fluids from valid output or IO ports.
    - Use redstone and comparators where supported to control or measure stored fluid.
    - Place it near boilers, turbines, or solar systems when a loop needs a large buffer.
