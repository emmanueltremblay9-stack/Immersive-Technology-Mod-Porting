Alternator
Rotational power to Flux
<&multiblock>The Alternator converts rotational power from a Steam Turbine or Gas Turbine into IF energy.

Connect the rotor input to the mechanical output of a turbine, then route energy from the Alternator's output connectors.<np>

§lOperation§r
    - Requires a valid mechanical power provider on its rotor input.
    - Produces energy while the connected turbine is running.
    - Higher turbine speed and torque improve generation; check the machine UI and JEI category displays for current values.
