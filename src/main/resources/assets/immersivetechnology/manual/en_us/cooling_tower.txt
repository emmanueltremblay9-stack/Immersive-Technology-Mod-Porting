Cooling Tower
Recover water
<&multiblock>The Cooling Tower condenses Exhaust Steam back into water. It helps close the loop after a Steam Turbine.

Provide a water feed and Exhaust Steam, then extract the recovered water from the outputs.<np>

§lOperation§r
    - Pipe water into the water input.
    - Pipe Exhaust Steam into the steam input.
    - Pull water from the output ports.
    - JEI lists the exact water and Exhaust Steam amounts for the cooling recipe.
