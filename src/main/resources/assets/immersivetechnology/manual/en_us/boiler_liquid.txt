Boiler Liquid Fuel Burner
Liquid-fired heat
<&multiblock>The Liquid Fuel Burner supplies heat to an attached Boiler Tank. It does not make steam by itself; the tank performs the water-to-steam recipe.

Feed valid liquid fuel into the burner and ignite the top exhaust area with a torch or flint and steel.<np>

§lOperation§r
    - Uses liquid fuels such as biodiesel, diesel, gasoline, and kerosene when those recipes are available.
    - Consumes a small pilot amount while lit but idle.
    - If steam is not producing, check fuel, ignition, redstone state, and the connected Boiler Tank.
