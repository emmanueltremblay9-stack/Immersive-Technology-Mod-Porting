Solar Reflector
Aim sunlight at a receiver
<&multiblock>The Solar Reflector directs sunlight into a Solar Tower or Solar Melting Crucible.

Reflectors only count when they are in range, at the proper height, and pointed at a valid receiver.<np>

§lOperation§r
    - Place reflectors at the receiver's collector height, not above or below it.
    - Keep reflectors 12 to 22 blocks from the Solar Tower or Solar Melting Crucible.
    - A receiver can use up to 24 reflectors.
    - Build in open sky for the best daytime heat gain.
