Boiler Solid Fuel Burner
Solid-fired heat
<&multiblock>The Solid Fuel Burner supplies heat to an attached Boiler Tank. It does not make steam by itself; the tank performs the water-to-steam recipe.

Insert valid solid fuel below the combustion chamber and ignite the top exhaust area with a torch or flint and steel.<np>

§lOperation§r
    - Uses solid fuels such as coal and coal coke when those recipes are available.
    - Consumes a small pilot amount while lit but idle.
    - If steam is not producing, check fuel, ignition, redstone state, and the connected Boiler Tank.
