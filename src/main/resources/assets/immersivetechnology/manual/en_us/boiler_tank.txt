Boiler Tank
Water to steam
<&multiblock>The Boiler Tank is the vessel at the center of the boiler system. It receives heat from an attached Liquid Fuel Burner or Solid Fuel Burner and turns water or distilled water into steam.

Feed water into the tank input and pull steam from the steam output.<np>

§lOperation§r
    - Must be placed so a Boiler Burner can supply heat to the tank.
    - Valid recipes include water and distilled water; JEI lists exact amounts and timings.
    - If steam is not producing, check the burner fuel, ignition, redstone state, and the tank's water supply.
